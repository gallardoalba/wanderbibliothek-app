Wanderbibliothek is a library driven by its community: the books already on your
shelf find new readers nearby, and what others are reading is just around the
corner.

· Share instead of hoard: list your copies and see who is looking for them.
· People instead of algorithms: swap with neighbours, not with a warehouse.
· Reading circles and lists: meet up, vote on the next book and keep writing
  together.
· Private stays private: messages and handover details are end-to-end encrypted;
  whoever runs the platform cannot read them.
· Free, without ads and without tracking.

Wanderbibliothek is an open project: the code is public, your data is yours, and
you can export or delete your account at any time.
